# AetherVoice: Cross-Chain Legal Framework

By using **KIRAPAY**, AetherVoice addresses the "Chain Fragmentation" problem in digital trade. 

### Legal Reliability:
- **Chain Abstraction:** KIRAPAY allows any asset to be used for payment, but AetherVoice ensures the **Legal Intent** is verified locally first.
- **Immutable Audit:** The metadata stored in the KIRAPAY intent (the QVAC hash) serves as a permanent record of the "State of Trust" at the time of payment.
- **Cross-Border Admissibility:** By using local-first AI, AetherVoice complies with cross-border data residency laws (GDPR/DPDP) because trade secrets never leave the merchant's device.
